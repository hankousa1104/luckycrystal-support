常見問題

怎麼恢復購買？

換手機、重新安裝，或在另一台 iPhone 上使用時，先用購買時的 Apple 帳號登入 App Store，再到 App 裡點「恢復購買」。不需要註冊本 App 的帳號。

「恢復購買」在這些地方都有：「商店」頁右上角、「我的」頁的「購買」區，以及每個解鎖視窗。系統可能會要求你輸入 Apple 帳號密碼。

可以和家人共享嗎？

目前不支援家人共享：購買的商品屬於購買時使用的 Apple ID。同一個 Apple ID 的 iPhone 都可以點「恢復購買」找回。

如果家長設定了孩子購買前要先經過同意，商品會先顯示「等待核准」，家長同意後就會自動解鎖。

指南針不準怎麼辦？怎麼校準？

手機指南針容易受磁鐵、喇叭、金屬桌面、磁吸手機殼或支架干擾。「今日」頁會顯示「精確度 ±N°」：

  • 誤差太大（大約超過 ±25°）時，畫面會提示校準：手持手機，在空中慢慢畫幾次「8」字，直到提示消失。
  • 附近有強磁場時，畫面會提醒你遠離後再看。
  • 每個方位佔 45°，對準方位中心 ±4° 以內，會顯示「已對準」。

本 App 不適合用於導航、登山、航海等需要精確方向的場合。

磁北和真北有什麼不同？要不要開定位？

App 預設使用磁北，不需要定位權限。磁北和地理上的真北之間有一個夾角，叫磁偏角。依世界地磁模型 WMM2025 推算（2026 年底）：台北約西偏 5.1°、香港約西偏 3.3°，相對每個方位 45° 的範圍影響很小；北美比較大，例如紐約約西偏 12.5°、西雅圖約東偏 14.9°，建議切換到真北。

在「今日」頁切換到真北時，App 會請求「使用 App 期間」的定位，由系統在手機上換算磁偏角。我們不儲存、不上傳你的位置；切回磁北就會停止定位。拒絕定位也能正常使用，會繼續按磁北顯示。之後可以在 iPhone 的「設定」→「隱私權與安全性」→「定位服務」裡修改。

怎麼加入主畫面小工具？
  1. 長按主畫面空白處，直到圖示開始晃動。
  2. 點左上角的「+」（iOS 18 以後是點「編輯」→「加入小工具」）。
  3. 找到「幸運水晶」，選擇小或中尺寸，點「加入小工具」。
  4. 長按小工具 →「編輯小工具」，可以選水晶（App 裡設定的那件、我的作品，或你擁有的任一顆水晶）和吉神。

想放自己做的作品：在 App 的「我的」頁點一件作品，設為小工具。小工具每天 0 點會自動換成當天的方位。

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

On a new phone, after reinstalling, or on another iPhone, sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account you used to buy, then tap Restore purchases in the app. You don't need an account with us.

Restore purchases is at the top right of the Store tab, in the Purchases section of the Me tab, and in every unlock sheet. Your iPhone may ask for your Apple Account password.

Can I share purchases with my family?

Not at the moment. Purchases belong to the Apple ID used to buy them. Any iPhone signed in with that Apple ID can get them back with Restore purchases.

If a parent uses Ask to Buy, the item shows “Awaiting approval” and unlocks automatically once the parent approves it.

The compass seems wrong. How do I calibrate it?

Phone compasses are easily disturbed by magnets, speakers, metal desks and magnetic cases or mounts. The Today tab shows “Accuracy ±N°”:

  • When the error is large (above about ±25°), the app asks you to calibrate: hold your phone and slowly draw a figure 8 in the air a few times until the message goes away.
  • If there is a strong magnetic field nearby, the app tells you to move away from it.
  • Each direction covers 45°. Within ±4° of the centre of a direction, the app shows “Aligned”.

Please don't use this app for navigation, hiking, sailing or anything else that needs an exact bearing.

What is the difference between magnetic north and true north? Do I need Location?

The app uses magnetic north by default, which needs no location permission. The angle between magnetic north and true (geographic) north is called magnetic declination. Based on the World Magnetic Model WMM2025 for late 2026, it is about 5.1° west in Taipei and 3.3° west in Hong Kong, which is small compared with the 45° each direction covers. It is larger in North America, for example about 12.5° west in New York and 14.9° east in Seattle, so we suggest switching to true north there.

When you switch the compass to True north, the app asks for location access "While Using the App" so the system can calculate the declination on your phone. We do not store or upload your location, and switching back to magnetic north stops location updates. If you decline, the app keeps working with magnetic north. You can change this later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services.

How do I add the widget to my Home Screen?
  1. Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap the + button in the top-left corner (on iOS 18 and later, tap Edit, then Add Widget).
  3. Find Lucky Crystal, choose the small or medium size and tap Add Widget.
  4. Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, and choose a crystal (the one set in the app, one of your pieces, or any crystal you own) and a deity.

To show a piece you made, open the Me tab, tap the piece and set it as the widget. The widget moves to the new day's direction at midnight.

  • Rough stones to saw, grind and polish in the workshop. A 1-piece rough makes one piece; a 3-piece rough makes three, two of them sister pieces.
  • Stands: rosewood stand, brass cradle and light-up stand, for your crystals and your own pieces.
  • Photo laser engraving: pick a photo, the subject is cut out automatically and engraved on the stone's surface.
  • Bowls: the pedestal bowl and the small bowl for DIY Display (the stone bowl is free).
  • Bundles: Golden Trio Roughs (citrine, rutilated quartz and green phantom roughs) and Two Bowls.
  • Complete Collection: every rough, stand, crystal, bowl and photo laser engraving available now. Items added later are sold separately.

Everything is digital content you buy once and keep. There are no subscriptions, no random items and no virtual currency. Items only change what you see and which stones you can use in the app; they have no supernatural, medical or health effect.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so we can't issue refunds ourselves and we never see your payment details. Please go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and make a request. Apple's rules and the law where you live apply. After a refund, the item is locked again in the app.
