隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 8 日

一句話版本

幸運水晶不需要註冊帳號，不收集、不上傳、不出售你的個人資料，沒有廣告和第三方統計。你做的作品和設定只儲存在你自己的手機上。

我們不收集的資訊

我們沒有伺服器，不收集姓名、電子郵件、電話、裝置識別碼、廣告識別碼（IDFA）、位置、通訊錄或使用紀錄，也不跨 App 追蹤你，因此不會跳出「允許追蹤」請求。

儲存在你手機上的資訊

  • 作品：你在原石工坊完成的作品（形狀資料、縮圖、同源編號、出材率），儲存在本 App 的文件目錄。
  • 小工具：你設為小工具的作品圖和名稱，儲存在本 App 與小工具共用的本機容器（App Group）裡。
  • 設定：所選吉神、磁北 / 真北等偏好。

這些資訊不會離開你的裝置（除非你自己用系統備份）。刪除 App 即全部刪除。

照片

你用「照片雷射雕刻」選的照片只在本機處理（去背、轉成雷射網點），不會上傳。作品裡儲存的是轉換後的雷射圖案，原圖不另外儲存；刪除作品就會一起刪除。

感測器與權限

  • 指南針（磁力計）：用於顯示朝向，不需要授權，讀數只在螢幕上使用。
  • 陀螺儀：用於水晶隨手機傾斜的光影效果，只在本機計算。
  • 定位（選用）：只有你在指南針頁切到「真北」時才會請求「使用 App 期間」的定位，系統用它換算磁偏角。我們不讀取、不儲存、不上傳你的位置；切回「磁北」即停止定位。你可以隨時在系統設定中關閉。

購買

App 內購買由 Apple 處理，我們拿不到你的付款資訊或 Apple ID。App 只在本機讀取 Apple 簽署的交易紀錄，用來判斷你擁有哪些商品。Apple 如何處理購買資料，請見 Apple 隱私權政策（apple.com/legal/privacy）。

第三方

本版本沒有接入任何第三方 SDK（統計、廣告、登入、雲端服務都沒有）。

兒童

本 App 面向 13 歲及以上使用者，不針對 13 歲以下兒童，也不收集任何兒童的個人資料。

你的權利

因為我們不持有你的個人資料，所以沒有可查閱、更正或刪除的伺服器資料。你可以在 App 內刪除作品，或直接刪除 App 清除全部本機資料。香港、台灣、歐盟 / 英國、加州等地法律賦予你的權利，同樣適用；如有疑問請聯絡我們。

跨境傳輸

本版本不向任何地方傳輸個人資料。

變更

如果以後加入需要連網或收集資訊的功能（例如雲端同步、AI），我們會先更新本政策並在 App 內告知，需要同意的會單獨徵求你的同意，不同意也不影響其他功能。

Privacy policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

In short

Lucky Crystal needs no account. We don't collect, upload or sell your personal information, and there are no ads or third-party analytics. Your pieces and settings are stored only on your own phone.

What we don't collect

We have no servers. We don't collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, device identifiers, the advertising identifier (IDFA), location, contacts or usage history, and we don't track you across apps, so you'll never see a tracking permission prompt.

What's stored on your phone

  • Pieces: the pieces you finish in the Workshop (shape data, thumbnails, same-stone IDs, yield) are saved in this app's Documents folder.
  • Widget: the image and name of the piece you set as your widget are saved in an on-device container shared by this app and its widget (App Group).
  • Settings: preferences such as your chosen auspicious deity and magnetic / true north.

This information never leaves your device (unless you back it up yourself with a system backup). Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Photos

Photos you pick for photo laser engraving are processed only on your phone (the subject is cut out and turned into laser dots) and are never uploaded. A piece keeps only the converted laser pattern, not the original photo; deleting the piece deletes it too.

Sensors and permissions

  • Compass (magnetometer): used to show which way you're facing. It needs no permission, and readings are only used on screen.
  • Gyroscope: used for the light effects as the crystal follows your phone's tilt; calculated on your device only.
  • Location (optional): the app asks for “While Using the App” location access only if you switch the Compass to “True north”; the system uses it to calculate magnetic declination. We don't read, store or upload your location, and switching back to “Magnetic north” stops location use. You can turn it off anytime in Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment information or Apple ID. The app only reads Apple-signed transaction records on your device to check which items you own. For how Apple handles purchase data, see Apple's Privacy Policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).

Third parties

This version does not include any third-party SDKs (no analytics, ads, sign-in or cloud services).

Children

This app is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect any personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we don't hold any of your personal information, there's no server data to access, correct or delete. You can delete pieces in the app, or delete the app to erase all data on your device. The rights you have under the laws of Hong Kong, Taiwan, the EU / UK, California and elsewhere still apply; if you have questions, please contact us.

Cross-border transfers

This version does not transfer personal information anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If we later add features that go online or collect information (such as cloud sync or AI), we'll update this policy first and let you know in the app. Anything that needs your consent will be asked for separately, and saying no won't affect other features.
